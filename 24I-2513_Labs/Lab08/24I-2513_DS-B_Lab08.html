Task 1: Top 10 richest countries

Prediction: I would expect the United States to be first in 2007 and also in 1952.

Top 10 countries in 2007

Income is GDP per capita, in inflation-adjusted international dollars.

Answers

  1. .slice(0, 10) keeps the first ten rows after sorting GDP per capita in descending order.
  2. .data(top10, d => d.country) uses the country as a key so D3 can match each bar to the same country when the year changes.
  3. The other bars look short in 1952 because Kuwait's GDP per capita is about 108,382, so it stretches the linear scale.

My prediction was wrong: Norway is first in 2007 and Kuwait is first in 1952, so the richest country changes.

The 2007 top ten contains countries from Europe, Asia and the Americas, with none from Africa.
