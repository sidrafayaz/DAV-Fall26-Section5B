Line Integrity: Discontinuous (No Interpolation)
Why an Unbroken Line Over Missing Data Is Misleading:

• False Certainty: Connecting two points across a 9-day blackout creates an artificial straight line, deceiving viewers into believing continuous measurements were recorded and stable.

• Hiding Events: March is a transition month with severe weather swings. A 9-day missing span could easily conceal a major smog spike or rain-clearing event.

• Proper Averaging: The 7-day moving average resets on March 21 and does not average across the gap. Averaging across the gap would corrupt post-outage statistics with stale readings from over a week earlier.
