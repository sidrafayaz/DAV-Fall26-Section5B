Step 2: Answers to Lab Task 2 Questions (One sentence each)

1. How many <rect> did you expect? How many did you get? Why?

We expected 142 <rect> elements (one for each country), but got 1,704 because gapminder.csv contains 12 survey years for each country (142 × 12 = 1,704 rows), drawing 12 overlapping bars per country.

2. How tall is one bar? Can you read the names?

Each bar is only about 2.5 pixels tall, causing all 142 country names and tick marks on the Y-axis to collide into an unreadable black smear.

3. After your first fix, is the chart easy to read? What would you change?

Filtering to a single year reduces the bars to 142, but 142 bars are still too cramped to read in a standard viewport; to make it readable, we should filter and rank to show only the Top 10 richest countries.

4. Now show the 10 richest countries. Is Norway in your chart? If not, what is missing?

If we sort all 1,704 rows without filtering by year, Norway is missing because historic Kuwait records from earlier decades occupy the top spots; what is missing is a filter condition for the year (d.year === 2007).
